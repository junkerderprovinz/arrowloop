The Android app gets the built-in VPN and finds your microSD card, run notes speak your language, and copies to MEGA are recorded again.

## ✨ Added

- **VPN in the Android app.** Settings › Integrations sets up a VPN from your provider or from your own WireGuard config, and a job picks the tunnel it goes through, as on a server or the desktop app. The tunnel runs inside ArrowLoop, so it needs no root and no Android VPN slot.
- **Timestamp tolerance on the phone.** A job on the phone can set its own timestamp tolerance, like any other value it can take from the global sync settings.
- **Two-factor sign-in for Internxt.** Adding an Internxt target runs its sign-in and asks for the code when the account has two-factor login on. Thanks to @Zeitsperre for the report ([#257](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/arrowloop/issues/257)).

## 🎨 Design

- **Notifications have a tile of their own.** The channels moved out of Safety net into their own settings tile. Thanks to @danpeig for the idea ([#254](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/arrowloop/issues/254)).
- **Connection rows follow GlimStone 4.8.1.** Buttons stand a step off the row, badges are a size smaller and move under a long name before it is cut, and wordmarks start where a symbol would.
- **No add button under Running.** The phone's overview leaves adding a job to the Jobs page.

## ⚡ Improved

- **Less memory in the tray.** A copy started straight into the tray now frees what the window used, and the tray icon only spins while a job runs. On Windows the app drops to a few megabytes once no window is open. Thanks to @danpeig for the careful measurements ([#256](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/arrowloop/issues/256)).
- **A job's database is named after its id.** New jobs keep their records in `state/<id>.db`, and existing ones are renamed once on start, so renaming a job no longer leaves an old name behind. Thanks to @danpeig for the suggestion ([#252](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/arrowloop/issues/252)).
- **Run notes read as whole sentences.** Every note in a run's details is a full sentence, and a file put off as written while copied names what changed, such as its time before and after. A file that grows after its last read during a local copy counts as written during the copy too, instead of as a failed copy.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Copies to MEGA are recorded.** MEGA keeps neither a file's modification time nor a checksum, so after an upload the time on MEGA is the moment of the upload. ArrowLoop compared it with the source file's time, found them apart and put every finished copy off as written during the copy, until the job reported an error. On a target without times it now compares the size, as rclone does, and a job stuck this way settles after one more run. Thanks to sipsip6770 on Discord for the very careful report ([#251](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/arrowloop/issues/251)).
- **The phone finds its microSD card.** The folder picker lists cards and USB drives under the name Android gives them, and leaves out "/", which Android does not let an app open. A refused folder now says why instead of showing "HTTP 400".
- **AppImage on the download page.** The docs' download section lists the AppImages for x64 and ARM. Thanks to @danpeig ([#255](https://github.com/junkerderprovinz/arrowloop/issues/255)).
- **Badges keep their descenders.** A long label in a run's file list no longer loses the bottom of letters such as g and p.

## 🌐 Translations

- **Run notes in every language.** The notes in a run's details are translated in the web app and on the phone, in all 42 languages, with numbers and times in your locale. The red message above a job's runs names its files in your language too, and so does a sign-in refused when a target is saved.
