ArrowLoop opens on an Overview, pairs your instances with twelve words, backs up its settings on its own, imports FolderSync and luckyBackup setups, trusts the certificate authorities you install on Android and comes as a Flatpak.

## ✨ Added

- **The Overview shows how everything stands.** It is the first page in the sidebar and where the app opens: one sentence on how things are, the running, paused and failed jobs, open conflicts, findings and the trash as tiles across the full width that each open their page, your jobs in your own order with their last and next run and Run now, the latest runs, and a card for each paired instance with its jobs, their last and next run and its latest runs, or when it was last seen while it is away. The new Sidebar card in Settings › Look shows or hides the Overview, Targets and Instances. On the phone, Overview on the bar under Appearance does the same, and Settings takes its place on the bar. Thanks to @Revan335 for asking in #127.
- **Your ArrowLoop instances can see each other.** Pair a server, the desktop app and the phone app with twelve words under Settings › Pairing, which looks and works as in BombVault and KnightLoader, with three animated steps. Instances then lists the jobs of the others with their last runs, their status, and Run now or Cancel the run. The instances approve each other before anything is shown, and Remove takes one out of the group everywhere, its key revoked for good. They meet on ParleyPort, the relay the project runs, on a relay of your own, or on one of your ArrowLoop servers that serves as the relay at `/relay/connect` for its own group only, and everything between them is encrypted end to end with a key made from the words, which never leave your instances. Thanks to @Revan335 for asking in #127.
- **ArrowLoop backs up its settings on its own.** Every night at 03:00 the engine copies `arrowloop.json` into a `backups` folder beside it if anything has changed, and again right before an import, and it keeps the newest seven. The Automatic backups card under Settings › General, and the same card in the phone's Settings, lets you switch it off and choose the days between two copies, the time of day, how many to keep and the folder. A device that was off at that time makes the copy when it is back. The card shows when the latest copy was made, and Restore puts it back through the import. A copy carries what an export carries, the notification secrets included, so only the account running ArrowLoop can read it. Thanks to @Revan335 for asking in #126.
- **Import takes a FolderSync export or a luckyBackup profile too.** The same Import button adds their jobs and targets beside yours, and first shows what comes along, what you still need to fill in and what stays behind. No password or token is carried over, and the new jobs arrive paused. An automatic backup of your setup is made just before. Thanks to @Revan335 for asking in #125.
- **The phone app trusts the certificate authorities you install on Android.** A server with a certificate from your own authority, such as a self-hosted cloud at home, connects once that authority is installed under Security in Android's settings. ArrowLoop reads the installed authorities each time its engine starts and again when you come back to the app, so a new one counts right away. Certificate checks always stay on. `docs/troubleshooting.md` shows how to install one. Thanks to @t4nki for asking in #129.
- **OxiCloud has a tile of its own under Clouds.** Its address field shows the WebDAV address OxiCloud documents, `https://your-server/webdav/`, and the password field says where OxiCloud makes the app password: Profile › App Passwords. Thanks to @t4nki for asking in #129.
- **ArrowLoop comes as a Flatpak.** Each release carries `arrowloop-linux-amd64.flatpak` and `arrowloop-linux-arm64.flatpak`, which bring their own WebKitGTK and open straight in your software centre. The Flatpak reaches your home folder and your drives under `/media`, `/run/media` and `/mnt`, starts with the system through the desktop's Background portal, and leaves updating to Flatpak, which the General tile says in place of Update automatically. Thanks to @Kasull-454 for asking in #131.

## 🎨 Design

- **Export settings and Import settings stand side by side** on the settings backup card, as in BombVault, and what the import takes is explained in its (i).
- **ArrowLoop follows GlimStone 4.2.8.** Moving a job, a settings tile or a column works as before, and the part that decides where it lands is the same one the other apps use. The phone app places a moved job with it too.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **The phone app's engine restarts quickly after you add or remove a certificate authority.** While a screen was open, the old engine waited ten seconds for it and then logged its exit as a failure. It ends the screen's live updates and stops right away, and a stop the app asked for is never logged as a failure.
- **A saved password in the phone app's target form shows its name again.** The row lost its label, the (i) sat on top of Saved and a wide empty space opened around it. It looks like the other fields: the name with its (i), and under it Saved and Change. The shared folders in the phone's settings keep their names readable beside Remove the same way.
- **The schedule choices in the phone app's job form keep their words whole.** At phone width Manual, Weekdays and Real time broke in the middle of the word. Each choice stays on one line, and the row wraps onto a second one where they don't fit side by side.
- **A Windows path typed into a phone folder no longer turns into a target.** Typing `C:/Program` on a side set to This phone made ArrowLoop read `C` as a target and show a third place called C. The side stays on the phone, and the field says there's no target called C and that folders on the phone start with `/`. A target's own name typed in front still moves the side to it.
