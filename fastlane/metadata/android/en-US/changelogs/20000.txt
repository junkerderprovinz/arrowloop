A new look built on GlimStone 3.5. The app opens on an overview with counts, the running jobs, the next and last runs and how full each target is, and a bar holds Overview, Jobs, Conflicts, History and More. A setting taken from the defaults shows its value and Override, and a stored password shows as Saved. There are more ways to be notified, notes on unusual runs, and a failed run says why in your language.
